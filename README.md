# ONDEWO LOGOS & BRAND KIT

Official ONDEWO logos plus a complete, **AI-ready brand & design system** — so
anyone (a designer, a marketer, or an AI coding agent) can produce on-brand ONDEWO
material just by pointing at this repo.

Logos publicly available under the official ONDEWO 1.0.0 Copyright Licence (for github repos for example).

---

## 🎨 Brand Kit

Everything to design and write on-brand:

- **[`brand-kit/`](brand-kit/)** — guidelines, design tokens, templates, clean logo SVGs
  - [Brand foundation](brand-kit/brand-foundation.md) · [Voice & tone](brand-kit/voice-and-tone.md)
  - [Logo guidelines](brand-kit/logo-guidelines.md) · [Colour system](brand-kit/color-system.md) · [Typography](brand-kit/typography.md)

- **[`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md)** — entry point for AI coding agents
- **[`.claude/skills/`](.claude/skills/)** — `create-brand-asset`, `brand-check`, `brand-copy`
- **[`.claude/workflows/`](.claude/workflows/)** — `campaign-kit`, `brand-audit`

### For AI agents
Point your agent at this repo and ask it to design something for ONDEWO. It reads
[`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) → [`brand-kit/`](brand-kit/) and produces correct,
on-brand output (right logo, colours `#004C87` / `#BE216B`, Futura/Jost type).

### Brand at a glance
| Blue | Magenta | Type | Tagline |
|------|---------|------|---------|
| `#004C87` | `#BE216B` | Futura → Jost | *We automate your phone calls.* |

## 🖼️ Logo source files

Official vector/raster logos in every format (`.ai .eps .pdf .svg .png .jpg .psd`,
RGB + CMYK) live in the repo root and the `ONDEWO_Logo_*` folders. The cleaned,
web-ready SVGs are in [`brand-kit/assets/logo/`](brand-kit/assets/logo/). See the
full [asset index](brand-kit/assets/ASSET-INDEX.md).

The original brand guideline: [`ondewo_logo_identity_guideline_2019.pdf`](ondewo_logo_identity_guideline_2019.pdf).
