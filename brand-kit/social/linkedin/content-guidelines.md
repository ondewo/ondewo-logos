# LinkedIn — content guidelines

## Tone

Insightful, professional, lightly bold. One idea per post, short sentences, white space between thoughts.

Personality dials (0–10): formality 7 · warmth 6 · boldness 5 · technicality 7 · playfulness 3.

## Hook / headline starters

- Hold music, retired.
- Answer every call, around the clock.
- A stat or a pointed question about call volume.

## Do

- ✅ Lead with a stat, a retired-pain line or a pointed question
- ✅ Link in the post or first comment, one verb-first CTA
- ✅ Add alt text to every image (‘ONDEWO …’ describing what is shown)
- ✅ Tag partners and customers only with written permission

## Don't

- ❌ Use banned hype words (revolutionary, disruptive, magic, world-class) without proof
- ❌ Post a wall of hashtags or engagement-bait polls
- ❌ Cut the logo off or use the white-background avatar on a photo

## Always

- `ONDEWO` in all-caps in prose; headlines and CTAs in sentence case
- Blue base, magenta as a single accent; never blue text on magenta or magenta on blue
  ([accessibility](../../accessibility.md))
- Use the official logo files from [`assets/logo/`](../../assets/logo/); never stretch, recolour or add effects
- Run the banned-word check from [voice-and-tone](../../voice-and-tone.md) before publishing
- No personal data, customer calls or unreleased claims; numbers need a source
