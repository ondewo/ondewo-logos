# Facebook — content guidelines

## Tone

Friendly-professional and concrete. Shorter than LinkedIn; lead with the benefit.

Personality dials (0–10): formality 7 · warmth 6 · boldness 5 · technicality 7 · playfulness 3.

## Hook / headline starters

- Waiting on hold is obsolete.
- See ONDEWO live: join our next webinar.
- Every call answered, in any language.

## Do

- ✅ Use the event cover for every webinar
- ✅ Keep captions short, link in the post
- ✅ Reply to comments within one working day

## Don't

- ❌ Paste LinkedIn copy unchanged
- ❌ Place text in the outer 90 px of the cover (mobile crop)
- ❌ Use more than 2 hashtags

## Always

- `ONDEWO` in all-caps in prose; headlines and CTAs in sentence case
- Blue base, magenta as a single accent; never blue text on magenta or magenta on blue
  ([accessibility](../../accessibility.md))
- Use the official logo files from [`assets/logo/`](../../assets/logo/); never stretch, recolour or add effects
- Run the banned-word check from [voice-and-tone](../../voice-and-tone.md) before publishing
- No personal data, customer calls or unreleased claims; numbers need a source
