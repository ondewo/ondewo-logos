## 1. What ONDEWO does

ONDEWO builds **conversational AI that automates voice and phone interactions** —
so organisations can answer every call, in any language, around the clock, without
growing their contact-centre headcount.

**Tagline (official):** *We automate your phone calls.*

The portfolio is modular — each product is a building block of an automated voice
stack:

| Product | What it is |
|---------|------------|
| **ONDEWO CAI** | Conversational AI engine — intents, entities, dialogue flows, the "brain" of the bot. |
| **ONDEWO S2T** | Speech-to-Text — high-accuracy, on-prem-capable transcription. |
| **ONDEWO T2S** | Text-to-Speech — natural synthetic voices. |
| **ONDEWO VTSI** | Voice/Telephony Service Integration — connects the AI to real phone lines (SIP). |
| **ONDEWO AIM** | Agent / interaction management & tooling. |

> The shared idea across all of them: **turn a phone call into software.**

## 2. Positioning statement

> For **enterprises and contact centres** that handle high call volumes,
> **ONDEWO** is a **conversational-AI platform** that **automates phone
> conversations end-to-end** — unlike generic chatbot tools, ONDEWO is
> **voice-first, multilingual, and deployable on-premise** for full data
> sovereignty. `‹confirm›`

## 3. Mission & vision `‹confirm›`

- **Mission:** Make every phone conversation answerable — instantly, in any
  language — by giving organisations production-grade voice AI they control.
- **Vision:** A world where waiting on hold is obsolete and no caller goes
  unanswered.

## 5. Brand personality

ONDEWO sounds like a **precise, calm, expert engineer** — not a hype-driven startup.

| We are | We are not |
|--------|------------|
| Clear & precise | Jargon-heavy or vague |
| Confident & credible | Boastful or over-promising |
| Human & approachable | Robotic or cold (ironic for voice AI) |
| Engineering-led | Buzzword-led |
| European, data-respecting | Careless with privacy |

**Personality dials** (0–10): Formality **7** · Warmth **6** · Boldness **5** ·
Technicality **7** · Playfulness **3**.

## 6. The brand idea behind the mark

The ONDEWO logomark is a **two-tone interlocking swirl** forming the "O" of the
wordmark. Read it as:

- **Two voices in conversation** — the magenta and blue strokes flow into each
  other (human ↔ machine, caller ↔ AI).
- **A sound wave / vortex** — motion, voice, signal.
- **Continuity & automation** — an endless loop that never drops a call.

Keep this metaphor in mind when generating imagery: **flow, dialogue, sound,
continuity, two elements completing each other.**

## 7. Messaging pillars `‹confirm›`

1. **Voice-first automation** — built for phone calls, not retro-fitted chat.
2. **Any language, any volume** — multilingual, elastic scale.
3. **You own your data** — on-premise / private-cloud deployment.
4. **Production-grade & developer-friendly** — real APIs, SDKs, uptime.
